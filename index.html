fly.io: a free endless flying game

Steer a low-poly plane through hills and obstacles that stream in forever. Every clean stretch builds a no-crash multiplier, every crash costs a life, and your best score is saved between visits. It runs in any modern browser with nothing to download, on desktop, on phones and in VR on Meta Quest.

Worlds

  • Dusk: sunset sands, long shadows and black monoliths.
  • Meadow: a voxel village on green hills with trees, cottages, hedges, clouds and sheep.

Planes

Choose from 20 planes, each with its own agility, armor and size: Dart, Manta, Needle, Falcon, Kite, Arrow, Boomer, Stiletto, Heron, Viper, Titan, Wisp, Raptor, Swan, Clover, Puff, Lamb, Sparrow, Zephyr and Aurora.

How to play

  • Desktop: arrow keys or A and D to steer, Space or Enter to start, C to change camera, P to pause, M to mute.
  • Phone: hold the left or right half of the screen to steer.
  • Meta Quest: open this page in the Quest browser and press PLAY IN VR, then steer with the thumbstick.
